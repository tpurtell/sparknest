every Spark rail (rocep1s0f0, roceP2p1s0f0) 200 Gb/s ACTIVE; raptor mlx5_0
400 Gb/s. Model: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8 (62 files, 34.9 GiB), sealed, source
copy warm in the source's page cache; readers' mounts freshly restarted
(cold). io_uring on.

writes); single-host reads are bound by the FUSE path at ~6–7 GB/s.

## Found and fixed

raptor first read at 0.56 GB/s: as the scratch archive's gateway it read the
model from its local SATA archive copy instead of the live copy on ostrich.
Reads now prefer a live copy elsewhere and use a local archive copy only as
the fallback (`own_read_source` in crates/nest-data/src/vfs.rs, test
`gateway_prefers_live_copies_over_its_archive`). After the fix raptor read
at 4.2 GB/s alongside five other readers, and 15 GB/s alone.
